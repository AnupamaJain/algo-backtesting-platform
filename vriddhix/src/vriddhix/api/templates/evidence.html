Method

Stated so the number can be argued with. A failure rate without a method is a slogan.

Universe
NSE cash equities that also have a listed future or option — the standard definition of the liquid Indian market.
Bias: membership is backfilled from today's constituent list, so this is the set liquid now, not then. That flatters the result and the platform says so everywhere it reports one.
What counts as a breakout
A close above the pivot of a detected volatility-contraction base, where the pivot is the high of the most recent contraction.
What counts as a failure
A close back below the pivot, beyond a small buffer. Close-based, not intraday — a wick through the pivot on a thin open is not a failed breakout, and counting it as one would inflate this number.
Look-ahead
Every detection used only bars dated on or before the day it ran, verified by truncating history and requiring identical output. The outcome is measured afterwards, which is a retrospective measurement rather than foresight.
