The sweep

The same {{ "{:,}".format(ten.tested) }} parameter configurations — {{ ten.swept|length }} strategies across {{ ten.symbols }} symbols — were walked forward over two windows of global market history: ten years, and then the most recent five. Three years in sample, one out, rolled annually. Costs and slippage applied. Six gates, the last of which is a false-discovery-rate correction.

Over ten years, {{ "{:,}".format(ten.survivors) }} configurations cleared every gate. Over five, {{ "{:,}".format(five.survivors) }} did.

Why the shorter window is harsher

It is tempting to read the second number as five years being a tougher market. It is mostly not. Look at where the configurations are lost.

The first gate asks only for thirty out-of-sample trades, and the five-year window loses most of its field there: half the history means roughly half the trades, and a strategy that trades rarely simply cannot show enough of them. That is a sample-size effect, not a verdict on the strategy.

The last gate is the interesting one. Over five years, {{ "{:,}".format(five.stages[4].survived) }} configurations reached the false-discovery-rate correction and {{ "{:,}".format(five.stages[4].survived - five.stages[5].survived) }} were rejected by it. Sweep ten thousand configurations and some will look excellent by luck; the correction is what stops the luckiest being reported as findings. On the ten-year window it rejects none, because by then the evidence per configuration is strong enough to stand.

What survived, and what did not

Of {{ ten.swept|length }} strategies swept, {{ ten.strategies|length }} produced at least one survivor over ten years and {{ five.strategies|length }} over five. The rest had every configuration rejected.

And the comparison that matters

A funnel tells you what passed a test. It does not tell you whether passing was worth anything. In both global windows the portfolio built from the survivors is beaten by buying the index and waiting: ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(g.starting_capital) }} becomes ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(ten.portfolios[2].capital) }} held against ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(ten.portfolios[0].capital) }} traded, over ten years.
