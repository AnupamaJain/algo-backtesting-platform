What an account does not do

It does not connect to your broker, place orders, or hold funds. VriddhiX reads market data and records what it measured; it has no trading path of any kind.

What we store

email
Identifies the account and scopes your watchlists, screens and alerts. Nothing else is derived from it.
password
Hashed with scrypt and a per-account random salt. The plaintext is never stored, never logged, and cannot be recovered — only reset.
your lists
Watchlists, saved screens and alert definitions. Filtered by the token on every request, never by a parameter a client could change.
